Road Damage Intelligence System

Automated Road Damage Detection using Computer Vision

Detect, Classify & Analyze Road Damage

This system uses YOLO object detection to automatically identify road damage in images and videos. It detects four types of damage from the RDD2022 dataset: Longitudinal Cracks (D00), Transverse Cracks (D10), Alligator Cracks (D20), and Potholes (D40).

Image Analysis

Upload road images for instant damage detection with bounding boxes, confidence scores, and severity estimation.

🎥

Video Analysis

Process video footage frame-by-frame with object tracking to identify and deduplicate damage events.

🔍

Damage Classification

Classifies damage into four RDD2022 categories: Longitudinal Crack, Transverse Crack, Alligator Crack, and Pothole.

⚠️

Severity Estimation

Visual severity heuristic based on bounding-box area, damage type, and confidence. Low / Medium / High levels.

🗄️

Detection History

All analysis sessions and detections are stored in a database for review and evidence retrieval.

Analytics Dashboard

Charts and statistics generated from real detection data: damage distribution, severity, confidence, and performance.
